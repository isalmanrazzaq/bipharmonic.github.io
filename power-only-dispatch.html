Questions about power only dispatch

What does power only dispatch cost?

Power only runs on one plan: $49 per week per truck plus 4% of weekly revenue, for 1 to 5 trucks.

Can I turn down loads?

Yes. There is no forced dispatch. You can decline any load without penalty, and you are always in control.

How many trucks and which states do you cover?

We dispatch 1 to 5 trucks in all 48 contiguous states.

Do you help with paperwork and money matters?

Yes. We handle load paperwork and assist with bookkeeping, accounts receivable and factoring support.

How do I get started?

Go to the sign-up page and send your name, phone number and the service you want. You will be introduced to your personal Built-in Partner, who starts work as soon as your paperwork is in.
